# **Company Overview**

Greenride Africa Tech Limited is a Kenya-based electric mobility company dedicated to accelerating the adoption of clean, affordable, and reliable electric transport solutions across the region. The company operates across the full electric vehicle (EV) value chain, from the wholesale and retail sale of brand-new electric vehicles, electric tuk-tuks, and light electric commercial vehicles, to second-hand EV trading, EV import/export, charging infrastructure, and technical consultancy.

By combining vehicle sales, fleet solutions, charging infrastructure, and after-sales support under one roof, Greenride Africa Tech positions itself as a one-stop partner for individuals, businesses, and fleet operators making the switch to electric mobility in Kenya and the wider East African region. The company is built to serve every stage of the EV ownership journey: helping a first-time buyer choose the right vehicle, helping a fleet manager plan a phased transition, and helping a property owner install the charging infrastructure that keeps it all running reliably and affordably.

As Kenya's transport sector shifts away from fossil fuels, driven by rising fuel costs, government policy support for e-mobility, and growing environmental awareness; Greenride Africa Tech is positioned to be a trusted, knowledgeable partner for anyone making that transition, from boda boda and tuk-tuk operators to corporate fleet owners.

# **Service Details**

## **1. New & Second-Hand Vehicle Sales**

Greenride Africa Tech supplies brand-new electric vehicles, electric tuk-tuks, and light electric commercial vehicles to individual buyers, businesses, and dealers across Kenya, on a wholesale and retail basis. The company also runs a dedicated second-hand EV desk, offering fair, transparent vehicle valuation and trade-in services for customers looking to upgrade or exit their current EV.

## **2. Import, Export & Spare Parts**

The company imports complete EV units and EV spare parts from established manufacturers and suppliers, and exports vehicles and parts within the region where opportunities exist. This gives customers and partner workshops reliable access to genuine components, reducing vehicle downtime and extending the working life of every EV on the road.

## **3. Fleet Solutions & Leasing**

For businesses transitioning company fleets to electric power, Greenride Africa Tech provides fleet sales consultation, helping organisations select the right vehicle mix, plan rollout, and estimate total cost of ownership. Flexible leasing arrangements are also available for customers who prefer to avoid the upfront capital cost of vehicle ownership.

## **4. Registration & Compliance**

Navigating NTSA registration can be one of the more time-consuming parts of buying a vehicle. Greenride Africa Tech assists customers through the registration process, helping them get their vehicles legally on the road with minimal delay.

## **5. Charging Infrastructure & Batteries**

The company sells EV charging equipment and lithium batteries, and develops and operates a network of AC and DC EV charging stations, a critical piece of infrastructure as EV adoption grows across Kenya. Greenride Africa Tech offers both charging technologies to match different vehicle types and dwell times, across our growing network of AC and DC charging stations, Greenride Africa Tech is committed to reliable, affordable charging; consistent uptime, transparent pricing, and consistent service quality at every location, whether it's a single home charger or a multi-bay public station:

- **AC Charging (Level 2)-** wall-mounted and pedestal chargers, typically 7kW, ideal for homes, offices, apartment blocks, and other locations where vehicles park for several hours, delivering a steady, cost-effective overnight or workday charge.

- **DC Fast Charging-** high-power chargers, from around 20kW up to 120kW and above, deployed at malls, fuel and service stations, highway rest stops, and fleet depots, topping up a vehicle in well under an hour for customers and operators who need a fast turnaround.

Beyond equipment sales, Greenride Africa Tech handles full-cycle charging station development, from site assessment and installation to ongoing station operation, maintenance, and remote monitoring, supporting property owners, businesses, and public sites that want to install AC and DC charging points for customers, staff, or the public.

## **6. Technical Consultancy**

Greenride Africa Tech offers electric mobility technical consultancy to individuals, businesses, and institutions exploring the switch to EVs, covering vehicle selection, charging infrastructure planning, and broader new energy vehicle strategy.
